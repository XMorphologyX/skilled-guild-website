Midnight Season 1 – A Guild Built for Progression

Skilled is a 3-day semi-hardcore Mythic raiding guild on Mal'Ganis focused on building a stable, long-term Cutting Edge–capable roster. We value preparation, consistency, and execution, and aim to progress as a team rather than cycling players each tier.

We are active outside of raid and expect the same level of engagement from our members.

Current Focus

We are currently progressing through Midnight Season 1 with a focus on building a stable core roster capable of consistent Mythic progression.

Our goal is to establish a strong foundation and push toward Cutting Edge–level performance as the tier develops.
